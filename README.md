## 1. No personal data collected
The app does not require an account and does not collect, store or share personal data such as your name, email, location, contacts or device identifiers. It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

## 2. Data stored on your device
Your settings (default currency, language, theme and the list of assets on your home screen) are stored only on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

## 3. Internet connections
To show current prices, the app downloads public market data from:
- Frankfurter (frankfurter.dev) – exchange rates based on central bank reference rates
- Mudo Studio's data server – daily gold, silver and oil prices

These requests contain no personal information. As with any internet connection, the servers technically receive your IP address; Mudo Studio does not store or analyse it.

## 4. Data sources
World Bank (Commodity Price Data), U.S. Energy Information Administration via FRED, European Central Bank via Frankfurter. Prices are approximate and for information only. The app does not provide investment advice.

## 5. Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

## 6. Your rights
Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data to access, correct or delete. Under the GDPR you may still contact us with any questions or lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

## 7. Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
